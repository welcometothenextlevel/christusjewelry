The Christus Collection

Nine pieces drawn from Bertel Thorvaldsen's 1838 marble Christus, standing in the Church of Our Lady in Copenhagen — the resurrected Lord, arms open, in quiet welcome. Each design is carried in sterling silver, gold, or stainless steel.

“Kommer til mig” — Come unto me. The inscription carved at the statue's base, from Matthew 11:28, is the quiet promise behind every piece in this collection.
